Our services

What we do

We keep our services simple and practical. Most clients start with one or two, and we build from there.

Upward communication networks & suggestion systems

We set up simple, safe ways for staff to share ideas and concerns with leadership, like suggestion boxes, regular check-ins, and short surveys. Then we help leaders respond, so people know they were heard.

Internal newsletters & employee communication programs

We create newsletters, bulletin boards, and regular team updates that keep everyone in the loop. When people hear news from their own workplace first, there’s less room for rumors.

Leadership speeches & executive speaking coaching

We help owners and managers plan and practice what they want to say, from a weekly staff meeting to a big announcement. You’ll walk in prepared and still sound like yourself.

Culture-building workshops & team programs

We run hands-on workshops and team activities that help people get to know each other, agree on shared values, and work better together. Then we help you keep those habits going after we leave.

Short-term messaging for rough patches

When things get tense, like after a change in ownership or a wave of people quitting, we help you decide what to say, who to say it to, and when. Quick, honest communication keeps small problems from growing.

Website & video production

We make simple websites and short videos that share your message with your team or your customers. We built this site, too.

Our process

How we work

  1. Listen

    We talk with people at every level, one on one and in small groups, to learn what’s really going on.

  2. Plan

    Together, we pick the few changes that will make the biggest difference.

  3. Follow through

    We help put the plan in place and check back to make sure the new habits stick.
