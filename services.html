Our services

Most clients start with one or two. Here’s exactly what each one includes.

Short-term messaging for rough patches

When trust breaks down, people need to hear the right things, in the right order, fast. We plan what leaders say first and make sure it reaches everyone.

What you get

  • Key messages in a deliberate order (more on that below)
  • An all-staff email, also posted where staff without email will see it
  • A speech from the leader to every employee, recorded for anyone who can’t attend
  • In-person listening sessions in every department
  • A clear timeline, so every promise gets kept

Leadership speeches & executive speaking coaching

We write speeches and messages with leaders, then rehearse them until they sound honest and like the person giving them.

What you get

  • Speechwriting for big announcements and all-staff meetings
  • Rehearsal and delivery coaching
  • Writing coaching for clear, human emails and memos
  • Advice on timing and channel, so hard news never arrives as a passing memo

Upward communication networks & suggestion systems

We build dependable ways for employees to speak up to leadership, and make sure leaders answer.

What you get

  • Two ways to be heard: one in person or on paper, one online
  • Anonymous options in both
  • A named person or team who reads every submission
  • Clear criteria for judging suggestions, and rewards for good ideas
  • A way for every employee to hear back about what happened

Internal newsletters & employee communication programs

Regular, honest updates keep rumors from filling the silence.

What you get

  • A regular internal e-newsletter
  • Management updates, staff news, and answers to employee suggestions
  • Progress reports on the promises leaders have made

How we handle hard news

Hard news, in the right order

When a change has hurt trust, the order of the message matters as much as the words. People need to feel respected and heard before they’ll accept facts. Leaders need to take responsibility and act before they ask anything in return.

  1. Honor what came before

    Start by respecting the history people are proud of. Change shouldn’t erase it.

  2. Own the mistakes

    A sincere apology comes first, so the explanation doesn’t sound like an excuse.

  3. Replace rumors with facts

    Answer the questions people are actually asking, plainly.

  4. Act right away

    Make a few changes people can see this week, so the words mean something.

  5. Open more doors

    Give people more than one way to be heard, and make leaders easy to find.

  6. Move forward together

    Only then, ask everyone to help build what comes next.

Our process

How we work

  1. Listen

    We talk with people in every department, from the front desk to the owner’s office, to learn what’s really going on.

  2. Act quickly

    We help leaders speak up right away, with honest messages and changes people can see.

  3. Build lasting habits

    Newsletters, programs, and ways to give feedback keep trust growing after the first speech.

  4. Track progress

    We agree on owners, dates, and a budget, then watch participation, suggestions, and how fast leaders respond, and adjust.
